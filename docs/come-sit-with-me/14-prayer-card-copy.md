# Prayer Card Copy

## Front (Prompt)
Come Sit With Me

What would you like to entrust to the Lord today?

You may share:
- A prayer
- A petition
- A longing
- A thanksgiving
- A praise
- A lament

## Back (Process)
Your offering will be received with reverence.

These prayers rest in the urn until worship.

At our next worship service,
these prayers will be carried and lifted before God
by the gathered people of St. James,
especially as we gather in communion.

After worship, you are invited to sit with our community in a brief release rite.
We gather with incense, water, and your written prayers.
We hold a moment for open prayer,
then release these prayers through fire before the Lord.

Incense symbolizes prayer rising before God,
water recalls baptism,
and fire is the physical release of these entrusted prayers.

After the ceremony, ashes are returned to the urn
and stored reverently.

You are beloved.
