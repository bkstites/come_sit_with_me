## Draft "What Happens To Your Prayer" Copy
"Step One - Come As You Are: At Pride in the Park on June 20, you are invited to place what is on your heart into words and entrust it to the urn. Prayer, grief, gratitude, longing, a quiet cry for help. You do not have to explain your heart here. Your prayer rests in the urn until worship.\
Step Two - Carried To The Table: Your prayer is carried into our next worship service. As we gather at the table of communion, we lift these prayers before the Lord without naming names, honoring your privacy with tenderness and care. Even if no one else knows your story, Jesus does. In His presence, you are held with compassion, dignity, and hope.\
Step Three - Released Before The Lord: After worship, you are invited to sit with our community for a brief release rite. We gather with incense, water, and your written prayers, hold a moment of open prayer, and then release these prayers through fire before the Lord. Incense symbolizes prayer rising before God, water recalls baptism, and fire becomes the physical release of what was entrusted. After the ceremony, the ashes are returned to the urn and stored reverently."
