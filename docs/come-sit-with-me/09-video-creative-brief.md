# Video Creative Brief

## Working Title
Come Sit With Me: Who Jesus Is To Us

## Goal
Introduce Jesus through authentic testimony from St. James members and invite viewers into worship and prayer.

## Target Length
2-4 minutes (plus a 60-90 second cutdown)

## Core Story Arc
1. Jesus invites: "Come"
2. Real voices: who Jesus is to me
3. Honest welcome for the uncertain and wounded
4. Invitation to entrust and come worship

## Interview Prompts
- Who is Jesus to you?
- What has Jesus changed in your life?
- What would you say to someone hurt by church?
- What does God's love feel like to you?

## Visual Direction
- Natural light, warm faces, unhurried pacing
- Pre-service moments, greetings, sanctuary details
- Keep authenticity over perfection

## Deliverables
- Final horizontal video for web embed
- Captions file
- Transcript text
- Thumbnail image
- 60-90 second social cut

## Closing CTA (On-Screen)
"Come Sit With Me\nSt. James Lutheran Church\nSunday 10 AM - Wednesday 7 PM\nstjamesfolsom.org"
