# Signage Copy Set

## Main Table Sign
Come Sit With Me
A sacred invitation from Jesus.

If you are willing, you are invited to entrust a prayer,
petition, longing, thanksgiving, or praise to the Lord.

## Prayer Urn Sign
Entrust A Prayer
Write what is on your heart and place it in the urn.

These prayers rest in the urn until worship,
then are carried and lifted before God by the congregation.

## Gentle Participation Sign
No pressure. No requirement.
You are welcome to receive this invitation,
whether or not you submit a prayer.
