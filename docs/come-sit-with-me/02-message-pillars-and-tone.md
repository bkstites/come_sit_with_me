# Message Pillars And Tone Guide

## Master Theme
Come Sit With Me

## Meaning Of "Me"
In this campaign, "me" refers first to Jesus of Nazareth. St. James serves as witness and host.

## Pillars
1. Jesus Invites
- "Come." is the central Gospel movement in this campaign.

2. You Are Beloved
- Belonging is not earned before approach.

3. Bring What Is Real
- People may bring petitions, grief, gratitude, questions, and longing.

4. The Church Carries Prayer
- Entrusted prayers are borne by the congregation in worship.

5. Release In Faith
- Prayers are offered to God with trust, not control.

## Tone
Use:
- Warm
- Reverent
- Honest
- Gentle
- Hopeful
- Christ-centered

Avoid:
- Corporate language
- Programmatic jargon
- Manipulative urgency
- Triumphal or transactional promises

## Preferred Language
- Sacred invitation
- Entrust to the Lord
- Carried into worship
- Lifted before God
- Released in faith
- Come as you are

## Language To Use Carefully
- "Campaign" (internal use only)
- "Experience" (can sound transactional)
- "Psychological" framing in public copy
- "Guaranteed answers"

## Public Promise Language
"Your prayer will be received with reverence, carried into worship, and lifted before God by the gathered people of St. James."
