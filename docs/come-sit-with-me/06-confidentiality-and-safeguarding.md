# Confidentiality And Safeguarding Protocol

## Purpose
Protect dignity, privacy, and safety for everyone who entrusts written prayers.

## Collection Standards
- Prayer submissions may be anonymous by default
- No requirement for name, phone, or email
- Urn remains supervised or sealed at all times

## Handling Standards
- Designated custodians transport the urn after event close
- Submissions are stored in a secure location before worship
- Access limited to approved ministry leaders

## Use In Worship
- Do not read identifying details publicly
- If excerpts are used, remove identifying information
- Prefer collective themes over specific personal details

## Prohibited Uses
- No sharing prayer text on social media
- No forwarding content in group chats
- No using submissions for fundraising or list building

## Pastoral Care Escalation
If a submission includes immediate harm risk:
- Notify pastoral lead immediately
- Follow local safety and mandatory reporting requirements where applicable
- Document actions taken

## Volunteer Conduct
- Speak respectfully and avoid invasive questions
- Do not photograph submissions
- Keep all participant conversations confidential

## Participant Communication
"Your submission will be handled with reverence and confidentiality and carried into worship as prayer before God."
