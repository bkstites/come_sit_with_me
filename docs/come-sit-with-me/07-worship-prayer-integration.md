# Worship Prayer Integration Plan

## Goal
Carry entrusted submissions into congregational worship in a reverent, non-exploitative way.

## Recommended Placement In Service
1. Welcome and framing sentence
2. Prayers of intercession (primary location)
3. Optional silent prayer moment
4. Optional mention during offering or sending

## Framing Script (Leader)
"This week, members of our community entrusted prayers, petitions, gratitude, and longings to the Lord. Today, as we gather at the table of communion, we carry these offerings together and lift them into the presence of Jesus."

## Intercession Pattern
- Pray for healing and reconciliation
- Pray for those carrying grief and fear
- Pray for those seeking faith and belonging
- Pray for courage to receive and reflect Christ's love

## Silence
Allow 30-60 seconds of silence after intercessions.

## Congregational Response
"Lord, in your mercy, hear our prayer."

## Post-Service Transition Script
"After worship, you are invited to sit with our community for a brief release rite. We will gather together with incense, water, and your written prayers. We will hold a moment for open prayer, and then we will release these prayers through fire before the Lord. Incense symbolizes prayer rising before God, water recalls baptism, and fire is the physical release of these entrusted prayers. After the ceremony, the ashes are returned to the urn and stored reverently."
