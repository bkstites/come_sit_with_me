# Come Sit With Me - Campaign Brief

## Campaign Name
Come Sit With Me

## One-Sentence Thesis
A sacred invitation from Jesus of Nazareth, carried by St. James, for people who feel distant from church to entrust what is on their hearts and be held in the prayers of God's people.

## Primary Goal
Create a coherent, reverent path from first encounter at Pride to worship participation through invitation, prayer submission, and communal intercession.

## Audience
- People spiritually curious but hesitant about church
- People harmed, excluded, or disillusioned by religious institutions
- People open to prayer, healing, reconciliation, or renewed faith
- Local community members who may attend St. James worship

## Core Promise
If you entrust a prayer, petition, longing, gratitude, or grief, it will be received with reverence, carried into worship, and lifted to God by the gathered congregation.

## Core Messages
- Jesus says, "Come."
- You are beloved.
- You do not need to be fully ready to come near to God.
- The church can carry prayer with humility and faith.
- Reconciliation begins with honest welcome and shared worship.

## What This Is Not
- Not a sales funnel
- Not emotional manipulation
- Not a guarantee of instant outcomes
- Not institutional self-promotion

## Desired Outcomes
- Increased trust among people far from church
- Meaningful prayer participation at the Pride event
- Higher attendance at the next worship service
- Clear witness to the Gospel through word and action

## Ministry Posture
- Jesus-centered
- Hospitable
- Non-coercive
- Pastoral
- Reverent
- Transparent
