# Burning Ceremony Order Of Service

## Purpose
A brief post-worship release rite where the community gathers to sit together with incense, water, and written prayers. We hold a moment for open prayer, then release these entrusted prayers through fire before the Lord, saying together: I bring this to you; I release it into your care.

## Safety First
- Conduct outdoors or in approved fire-safe area
- Assign fire steward and extinguisher
- Use a stable vessel suitable for controlled burning
- Keep distance boundary for participants
- Follow local venue and fire regulations

## Symbols
- Incense: prayers rising before God
- Water: baptismal remembrance
- Fire: physical release of entrusted prayers before the Lord

## Suggested 10-15 Minute Order
1. Gathering sentence and invitation to sit together
2. Opening prayer
3. Brief scripture (optional: Psalm 141:2)
4. Silent reflection with incense
5. Incense offering
6. Water blessing gesture
7. Moment for open prayer (community may share if moved)
8. Controlled burning of submissions before the Lord
9. Return ashes to urn for reverent storage
10. Closing prayer and blessing

## Gathering Sentence
"We gather to release what has been entrusted, placing these prayers before the Lord with humility, hope, and faith."

## Opening Prayer
"Merciful God, receive these prayers and the people who offered them. Hold every longing, every grief, every thanks, and every cry for help in your love. Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen."

## Closing Blessing
"Go in peace. What has been entrusted is held by God."
