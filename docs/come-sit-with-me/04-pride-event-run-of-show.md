# Pride Event Run Of Show

## Objectives
- Offer sacred invitation through card + QR + conversation
- Receive prayer submissions in the urn
- Invite participation in upcoming worship

## Team Roles
- Host lead (overall flow)
- Invitation volunteers (2-4)
- Prayer station steward (1-2)
- Logistics and supplies (1)
- Pastoral support point person (1)

## Timeline Template
## T-90 minutes
- Set up table, signage, urn, cards, pens
- Prayerfully ground the team
- Review non-coercive invitation language

## Event open
- Greet and offer card
- Briefly explain invitation
- Offer optional prayer submission
- Offer worship service invite

## Mid-event pulse checks (every 60 minutes)
- Refill cards/pens
- Check volunteer energy
- Rotate roles
- Review any pastoral concerns

## Event close
- Seal urn respectfully
- Inventory remaining materials
- Debrief immediate observations
- Transport urn to designated custodians

## Debrief Questions
- What invitations felt natural and meaningful?
- Where did people hesitate?
- What language built trust?
- What should be changed before next event?
