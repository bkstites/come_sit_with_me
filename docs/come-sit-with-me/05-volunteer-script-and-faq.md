# Volunteer Script And FAQ

## Volunteer Opening Script
"Hi, we are from St. James Lutheran Church. We are sharing a sacred invitation called Come Sit With Me.\
If you want, you can take this card and scan the QR code.\
You are also welcome to write a prayer or longing and place it in our urn.\
These prayers rest in the urn until worship. Our congregation then carries them into our next service and lifts them to God."

## 10-Second Script
"Would you like a card? It's an invitation from Jesus: Come Sit With Me."

## If Someone Asks "Do I Have To Be Christian?"
"No. This invitation is open to anyone who wants to entrust something in prayer."

## If Someone Says "I've Been Hurt By Church"
"Thank you for sharing that. We are sorry for the harm you experienced. You are welcome to receive this invitation with no pressure."

## If Someone Asks "Will You Read My Card?"
"We treat all submissions with reverence. We do not publicly identify people, and we carry the prayers into worship as entrusted offerings. We will not read the prayers; we trust that your heart and God's presence are enough."

## If Someone Asks "What Happens In The Release Rite?"
"After worship, we gather in a brief release rite. Incense symbolizes prayer, water recalls baptism, and fire is the physical release of these entrusted prayers before the Lord. After the ceremony, ashes are returned to the urn and stored reverently."

## If Someone Wants Prayer On The Spot
"Yes, if you would like, I can offer a short prayer with you right now."

## Boundaries
- Do not debate
- Do not argue theology in passing conversations
- Do not pressure anyone to submit a card
- Do not promise specific outcomes from God
- Escalate acute distress to pastoral support lead
