## Tabernacle Supplies — Sacred Space Within The Tent

### Structure & Threshold
- Tent or canopy (outer boundary of the booth)
- Sheer or linen curtain panels to create an inner threshold — a visible "entering a set-apart place" moment
- Curtain rod, tension rod, or two poles and rope to hang the threshold veil
- Small mat or rug inside the space for people to stand or kneel on
- "Enter Here" threshold sign — simple, gentle, no pressure (e.g. "You are welcome to step in")

### Altar Table
- Small table (card table or folding table) covered with white or gold linen cloth
- The prayer urn — centered, visible, the focal point
- Small cross or icon positioned behind or above the urn
- Two pillar candles (battery-powered for safety) flanking the urn
- Small incense holder with incense sticks or cone — lit or unlit depending on wind and ventilation
- Small vessel of water (a simple bowl) representing baptism
- Cloth or runner in gold, white, or deep purple beneath the urn

### Prayer Card Station
- Printed prayer cards (front: prompt; back: process)
- Short pencils or pens (enough that several are always available)
- Small card stand or holder displaying a blank card as an example
- A written instruction sign: "Write what is on your heart and place it in the urn"
- A second card to keep — so the person leaves with the process description in hand

### Atmosphere & Lighting
- Battery-powered string lights or small LED candles to warm the space
- Small Bluetooth speaker for quiet, ambient instrumental or sacred music (low volume)
- Potted plant, greenery, or simple flowers to bring life into the space
- Soft fabric draped over the sides of the tent interior to soften the space
- A small printed scripture card on the altar: Psalm 141:2 — "May my prayer rise before you like incense"

### Signage Inside The Space
- Main sign on or near the urn (from doc 13): "Write what is on your heart and place it in the urn. These prayers rest here until worship."
- Small privacy sign: "This is a quiet, sacred space. You are welcome to stay as long as you need."
- Optional: a small printed card with Philippians 4:6-7 to read while writing

### Volunteer Presence
- One attendant stationed just outside the threshold — not inside unless invited
- Attendant role: gentle welcome, offer a card, answer questions, give people space
- Attendant should not watch people write — turn slightly away or tend to the outer table
