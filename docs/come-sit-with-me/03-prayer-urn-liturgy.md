# Prayer Urn Liturgy - Pride Event

## Purpose
Offer a simple, sacred action where attendees may entrust prayers and longings to Jesus.

## Suggested Spoken Invitation (30-45 seconds)
"If you would like, you are invited to write a prayer, a petition, a thanksgiving, a praise, or a longing and place it in this urn.\
We call this Come Sit With Me, because we believe Jesus still says, 'Come.'\
If you choose to share something, these prayers will rest in the urn until worship, and our congregation will carry them into our next service and lift them to God with reverence.\
No pressure at all. You are welcome to participate, or simply receive the invitation."

## Optional One-Sentence Version
"If you want, you can entrust a prayer to the Lord here; it will rest in the urn until worship, then be carried and lifted before God."

## Prayer Card Prompt
"What would you like to entrust to the Lord today?\
Prayer, petition, longing, gratitude, praise, or lament."

## Volunteer Guidance
- Offer, do not push
- Speak slowly and gently
- Let silence be okay
- Thank people for trusting the space
- Do not ask for details unless invited

## Closing Blessing For Participants
"May the Lord receive what you have entrusted, and may you be held in God's peace and love."
